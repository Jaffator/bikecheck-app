The page transitions were retuned

Section 6 of yesterday’s document describes the slide transitions as they stood at 19:30. Three files changed after that, so parts of that description are now out of date. What is on disk today:

Timing and easing changed together

Duration went from 260 ms to 480 ms, easing to cubic-bezier(0.16, 0.9, 0.55, 1). The reasoning is commented in place: the new curve finishes braking earlier, so the old budget would have left the panel sitting still at the end of its own animation. The comment records which way to push the second control point to lengthen or flatten the tail.

Layers now paint their own background

Pages carry no background of their own — it lives on the AppShell — so two bare sliding layers showed through each other in flight. A new surface prop paints each layer for exactly as long as it animates, transparent at rest so the shell background and footer gradient still show.

Same-route re-renders no longer restart the animation

When the pathname is unchanged but the children are not — a query resolving, a wizard step advancing — content swaps without starting a transition. An identity bail-out on the children stops it re-rendering on every parent render.

Arriving pages no longer fade

The inbound keyframes animate transform only. An arriving page is the layer the eye lands on, and a translucent one showed the outgoing page straight through it. The page underneath still dims to 0.6 as it gives way — that one is meant to recede.
